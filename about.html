Computer Vision Research Engineer · Software Engineer

I teach machines to see.

By day I'm a Computer Vision Research Engineer at LISER, the Luxembourg Institute of Socio-Economic Research. I work on MonWaDes, where Vision Transformers look at satellite images of cities before and after a war and find the buildings that were destroyed. It's research with real consequences, and it runs from raw imagery all the way to models training on a GPU cluster.

I'm also a software engineer at heart. I co-founded Cambrian, a software house, and lead Shifter, an app that builds hospital rosters automatically. Before that I shipped NLP and LLM systems at MantisNLP and built open-source tools like Sieves and Nervaluate.

Computer vision is where models meet the physical world, and software is how they get there. That's why my side projects usually have a camera on them: the latest is a self-driving robot car with LiDAR mapping and real-time object detection running on a Raspberry Pi.

If you're working on something that needs to see, let's talk.
